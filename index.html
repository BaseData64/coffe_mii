    About my projects

    PROJECTS

    "Back in 2024, I set out on what was perhaps my most ambitious technical project: attempting to recreate the Miiverse prototype originally presented by Satoru Iwata at E3. This was an immense challenge, as I had to hunt down rare, obscure materials across the internet. I spent weeks analyzing old screenshots, using logic and deduction to try and reverse-engineer features that were never officially released and whose functionality remained a mystery.

    I was dedicated to this for two months, working tirelessly to build a functional 'WUBH' environment that would allow others to experience this lost vision. I was only about a month and a half away from releasing the first public prototype. Unfortunately, disaster struck when my PC suffered a fatal system crash—the dreaded blue screen of death. I was forced to format my drive, losing not only the code but all the rare photographs and research materials I had gathered. The sheer exhaustion and frustration of losing two months of intense work without a proper backup was devastating. I ultimately decided to abandon the project, but the experience gave me a deep, unparalleled look into the Miiverse architecture that I'll never forget."

    My journey into modding began in 2023, and it has been a steady progression of learning and experimentation. What started as simple modifications quickly evolved into more complex and fun cosmetic projects. Over time, I managed to dominate the internal file logic of Wii U games, which allowed me to start creating much more advanced and professional-grade mods.

    The process of understanding how these games handle assets, textures, and logic has been incredibly rewarding. Currently, I am working on a custom, comprehensive pack for Mario Kart 8 Deluxe. This project is a collection of my favorite personal mods—things that are tailored specifically to my taste and playstyle. It’s a great feeling to take a game I love and completely customize it to be 'mine.' I’m really enjoying this creative freedom, and I look forward to continuing to push what is possible within the game's engine.
